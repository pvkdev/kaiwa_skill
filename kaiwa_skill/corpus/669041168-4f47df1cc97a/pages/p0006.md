### How to Use the CD

The enclosed CD contains all of the model conversations and conversation texts (Japanese) in the “conversation drills” section.

① First read all of the conversation texts listed on the two pages (or one page) for each pattern. Try to un-derstand the meaning of each and imagine where the conversations are taking place.

② Next, listen to the CD while you read the book. Pay close attention to the types of sounds spoken, intona-tions, and nuances in speech.

(Biên tập: un-derstand và intona-tions giữ dấu nối tại chỗ ngắt dòng tiếng Anh in trong nguồn.)

③ Listen to the CD while reading the text. Try to mimic the sounds aloud directly after they are spoken.

④ Now practice the same way again without looking at the book.

<a id="p0006-b003"></a>
### CD 的使用方法

附带的 CD 收录书中所有的会话原型和“会话练习”中的会话文（日语）。

① 首先，我们要学习第二页（或第一页）中所介绍的会话文，然后一个一个地具体理解会话的意思，并且想象会话的场面。

② 第二步，边看书变听 CD，边听边确认怎样发音、声调和声音的语感。

③ 边看书边听 CD，并且趁热打铁地进行模仿练习。

④ 最后，不要看书，进行同样的训练。
