## Key Dialogue tiếp theo (biên tập)

**Noda:** Not at all. It’s the fruit of the efforts of your staff. I had the pleasure of knowing Mr. Barner, the previous CEO. I remember that he had a lot of difficulties.

**Smith:** Yes. Mr. Barner is now division manager at our head office. Before I came to Japan, he told me a lot about the difficulties of the early years. I understand that you helped him quite a bit yourself.

**Noda:** Not at all. It’s just that I happened to know some people at MITI and the Ministry of Health and Welfare. I just dropped a word here and there.

**Smith:** Thanks to you our share in Japan has become quite stable.

**Noda:** That’s good news. I understand that among foreign companies you have the first or second largest share. It’s quite an achievement to be so successful in such a competitive field.

**Smith:** No, not at all. Incidentally, Mr. Noda, do you know the president of our company?

**Noda:** Yes, I do. When I went to Canada, I met him through Mr. Barner. He seems to have a technical background. I hear he’s quite active outside the pharmaceutical field.

**Smith:** Well, everyone is diversifying now.

**Noda:** Well, how is business recently?

**Smith:** Thanks to you, I’m getting used to doing business in Japan and am somehow managing.

**Noda:** I’m glad to hear that.

**Smith:** How are things with you?

**Noda:** Well, for a while our export division was hit hard by the stronger yen but (thanks to you) things are gradually returning to normal.

**Smith:** That’s splendid! By the way, I understand that you are quite fond of golf. Do you go often?

**Noda:** Yes, I do. You know what they say about fanatic novices. I just don’t seem to get any better.

JLPT Mogi

Yuuki Bui
