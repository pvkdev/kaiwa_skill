## Variation 1: Sharing gifts

A parcel had arrived for A from his home town, Aomori. B, his neighbor had accepted delivery during A’s absence so A now takes some apples to B in appreciation.

**A:** Thank you for taking care of the parcel for me yesterday.

**B:** Not at all.

**A:** These are some apples from Aomori. I’m afraid there are only a few, but please try them.

**B:** You shouldn’t have bothered. Can I really have all these?

**A:** It’s only a few.

**B:** Aomori is known for its apples. They look delicious.

**A:** Do you like apples?

**B:** Yes, very much.

<a id="p0053-b003"></a>
## Variation 2: A gift

A has been accepted by a company. He visits B to thank him for his help.A:

**A:** Thanks to you I have been hired. Thank you very much for everything. This is just a token of my appreciation.

**B:** Oh, no. You shouldn’t. You put me in an embarrassing position. I really haven’t done anything.

**A:** Well, I really want to do it.

**B:** You needn’t bother.

**A:** But you really have helped me.

**B:** Well, if you insist. I seem to have caused you a lot of trouble.
