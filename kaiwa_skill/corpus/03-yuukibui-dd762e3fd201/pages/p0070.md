## Lesson 14　Meeting People For The First Time

### Objectives

In this lesson you will study how to greet a person you are meeting for the first time. When you are visiting a company to meet someone you will have acquaintances in common with the person you are visiting. Here you will practice how to convey a message to the person you’re visiting, from your mutual acquaintance.

<a id="p0070-b002"></a>
### Key Dialogue: Meeting the company president

Shachō: the company president  
Kachō: a section chief  
Jones: a trainee from England

Mr. Jones has just arrived from England as a new trainee. The section chief introduces him to the president.

**Kachō:** Shachō, let me introduce Mr. David Jones, the trainee (we talked about). It’s been decided that he’ll work in the Import Section.

**Jones:** I’m David Jones, how do you do?

**Shachō:** Have you gotten used to life in Japan?

**Jones:** Yes, (thanks to you).

**Shachō:** How about the (Japanese) language?

**Jones:** I can somehow manage (to speak) daily conversation. The Japanese language is becoming more interesting as I learn more words.

**Shachō:** I’m glad to hear that. You’re British, aren’t you? What sort of work were you doing before?

**Jones:** Mainly in the market research area.

**Shachō:** Really? The Japanese distribution system is a bit complex so it may be quite different from the way things are in Europe.
