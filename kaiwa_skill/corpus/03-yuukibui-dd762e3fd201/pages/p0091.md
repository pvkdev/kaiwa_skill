## Key Dialogue tiếp theo (biên tập)

Mr. Bell visits Okuyama’s office to meet him.

(At the receptionist’s desk)

**Recep.:** Good afternoon. (May I help you?)

**Bell:** (Bell hands his business card to the receptionist) My name is Robert Bell of Superior Tech Industries of Canada. I have a three o’clock appoint-  
ment with Mr. Okuyama, chief of the Sales department.

**Recep.:** Mr. Bell of Superior Tech Industries of Canada? We’ve been expecting you. Let me take you to the room (where we receive customers).

## Variation: Speaking to the manager in the hall way

A would like to discuss something with B, the section chief. He asks B to set aside some time for him.

**A:** Kachō, could you spare some time for me when you’re free? There’s something I’d like you to give me advice on.

**B:** Sure, fine. However, right now is. . .

**A:** There is no immediate hurry, so any time will do. It’ll only take about 30 minutes.

**B:** I’ll be through in about an hour. Speak to me then.

**A:** Thank you. And, I’d prefer not to talk about it in the office.

**B:** The office is inconvenient? Well then, shall we meet in the basement coffee shop?

**A:** (Yes) Sorry for holding you up. Thank you for making time for me.

**B:** Not at all. I’ll see you later.
