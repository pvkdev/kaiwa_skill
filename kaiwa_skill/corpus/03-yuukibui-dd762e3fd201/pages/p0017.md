## Key Dialogue 2: Asking someone to make a telephone call for you

Bonaan: a foreign employee

Inoue: Bonaan’s colleague

**Bonaan:** Mr. Inoue, would you do me a favor?

**Inoue:** Yes, what is it?

**Bonaan:** I’d like to send a Kutani vase to my aunt in France. Since it’s a very expensive item, I’d prefer to send it through a shipping company that specializes in art objects.

**Inoue:** Then I think Tōkyō Tsūun would be a suitable one to use.

**Bonaan:** I see. Would you mind asking them how much they would charge? Since I’m not confident enough with my Japanese yet.

**Inoue:** Sure. I’ll call them right away.

## Variation 1: Asking your colleague to do something (in the office)

**A:** Mr. Nakamura, sorry to bother you, but could you help me?

**B:** Sure, what is it?

**A:** Can you sort this data into those related to the U.S. and those related to Asia?

**B:** You want me to divide them into two categories, right?

<a id="p0017-b003"></a>
## Variation 2: Asking your superior to do something (in the office)

**A:** Would you mind taking a look at this paper?

**B:** O.K.

**A:** I’d appreciate it if you could put your personal seal on it, too.

**B:** O.K.
