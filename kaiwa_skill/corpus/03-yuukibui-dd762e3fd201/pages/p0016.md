## Lesson 3　Requests

### Objectives

In this lesson you will practice how to make requests. You will learn how to use the polite form used towards one’s seniors and the informal form used between friends.

<a id="p0016-b002"></a>
### Key Dialogue 1: Asking someone to make copies in the office

Kachō: a section chief

Nakamura: Kachō’s subordinate

The section chief asks his subordinate, Mr. Nakamura, to make copies of documents.

**Kachō:** Mr. Nakamura, can you spare a few minutes?

**Nakamura:** Of course.

**Kachō:** Can you make two enlarged copies of each of these files?

**Nakamura:** Yes, two copies each? Should I make covers for the files?

**Kachō:** No, that won’t be necessary because they’re just for use inside the office. Stapling will do.

**Nakamura:** I’ll do it immediately.

**Kachō:** Oh, since you’re going in that direction, could you tell the sales promo-  
tion section chief, Mr. Kimura, to come over to my desk?

**Nakamura:** Certainly.
