## Variation 2: When the person requested is on another line

**B:** I’m afraid Mr. Taniguchi is talking on another phone now. Can you wait? Or shall we call you back?

**A:** I’m speaking from a public phone now. Could you give him a message please?

**B:** Yes certainly. Go ahead please. (takes notes)

<a id="p0038-b002"></a>
## Variation 3: When the person requested is still talking on the other line

**B:** Mr. Taniguchi is on another line now. I think he’ll finish soon. Could you hold the line?

**A:** All right, I’ll wait a while. (waits)

**B:** I’m sorry to keep you waiting. It seems like it will take a while more. Shall I have him call you back later?

**A:** Yes, could you please have him do that?

<a id="p0038-b003"></a>
## Variation 4: When the other party says he’ll call back

**B:** Shall I have Mr. Taniguchi call you back when he returns?

**A:** No, that’s all right. I’m calling from outside (a public phone). I’ll call again.

**B:** Is that so? Could you please do that? I’ll inform Mr. Taniguchi about your call.

**A:** Thank you, please do that.

**B:** I understand and will do so.

<a id="p0038-b004"></a>
## Variation 5: When the other party hasn’t identified himself

**A:** Is Mr. Taniguchi there, please?

**B:** Excuse me. Who is calling please?

**A:** This is Yamada speaking.

**B:** Mr. Yamada of which company please?
