## Variation 4: A brief chat with a neighbor

A met his/her neighbor, B, on the street.

**A:** Hello. It’s hot today, isn’t it?

**B:** It is. Going somewhere?

**A:** Yes. Just out on an errand.

## Variation 5: Moving

B: a Japanese woman

A: John, an Australian

John [A] just moved into a new apartment. He goes to introduce himself to the people living in the flat below.

(doorbell)

**B:** Yes, who is it?

**A:** I’m your new neighbor, just moved in upstairs, number 405.

(opens the door)

**A:** My name is John Smith. I just moved in upstairs, in Apt. 405. How do you do? This is a small gift.

**B:** Thank you very much for your kindness. I’ll accept it with pleasure. You speak Japanese well. Where are you from?

**A:** I’m from Australia. Since I’m living right above you, if I make too much noise, please don’t hesitate to tell me.

**B:** All right. If I could be of any help to you, feel free to ask.

**A:** That would be nice. Thank you. Goodbye.
