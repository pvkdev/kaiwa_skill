## Variation 1: Refusing because one is too busy at that moment

A asks B(Mac)to help but…

**A:** Mac, could you help me?

**B:** Yes, what is it?

**A:** If you’re free, could you check this?

**B:** Sorry, I’m really busy at the moment. I can do it after twenty minutes or so.

<a id="p0022-b002"></a>
## Variation 2: Refusing an invitation to a baseball game

**A:** I have some tickets to a baseball game. Kyo-san would you like to come along?

**B:** You do? When is it?

**A:** It’s Thursday night. It’s a weekday, though, so maybe you can’t?

**B:** No, that’s not a problem, but unfortunately, I have an appointment already for Thursday evening.

**A:** Oh, that’s too bad.

**B:** Let’s try again some other time.

**A:** O.K. Some other time.

## Variation 3: Refusing if you don’t feel like doing something

**A:** Chin-san would you like to go to a Live House in Shinjuku today?

**B:** What kind of place is that?

**A:** Basically, it’s a place to have a drink and listen to live music.

**B:** What kind of music?

**A:** Jazz or rock’n’roll, usually.

**B:** Sorry, but I’m not so crazy about that kind of music.
