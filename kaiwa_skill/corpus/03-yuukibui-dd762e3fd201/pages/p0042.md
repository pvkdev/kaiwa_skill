## Lesson 8　A Company Trip

### Objectives

In a Japanese company there are often many different functions and events. In this lesson you will practice how to ask about these events, how to apply for them, and also how to fill out the application forms.

<a id="p0042-b002"></a>
### Key Dialogue: A company outing

Smith: a foreign employee

Nakamura: Smith’s colleague

There is a company outing being planned in Mr. Smith’s firm. He wants to join, and asks his colleague, Mr. Nakamura, about it.

**Smith:** Mr. Nakamura, I’d like to ask you about the company trip next week.

**Nakamura:** Oh! Didn’t you get a circular?

**Smith:** No, I haven’t seen it. When is the trip?

**Nakamura:** Next Saturday and Sunday.

**Smith:** Oh, it’s an overnight trip! Where are we going?

**Nakamura:** To Kusatsu Hotsprings.

**Smith:** Where is Kusatsu Hotsprings?

**Nakamura:** It’s on the border of Gumma and Nagano prefectures. We’re going on a chartered bus. You’re joining us, aren’t you?

**Smith:** Yes, but can I sign up now?

**Nakamura:** Yes, I believe the deadline is this Friday. Ask Mr. Yoshida of General Affairs.

**Smith:** Okay, I’ll do that. Oh, by the way, I understand that at a party during a company outing people are made to sing or entertain. Is it true? I’m tone deaf.
