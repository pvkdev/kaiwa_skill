## Listening Practice tiếp theo (biên tập)

**Nakamura:** Er. . . I’d like to make a reservation for tomorrow.

**Restaurant:** Thank you. Let’s see. . . That’s for the 6th. How many people are there in your party?

**Nakamura:** Ten.

**Restaurant:** I see. May I have your name please?

**Nakamura:** Nakamura of Tōyō Shōji.

**Restaurant:** Do you want a table?

**Nakamura:** No, I’d prefer a tatami room.

**Restaurant:** We’ll reserve a tatami room for you. What do you wish us to serve?

**Nakamura:** Can you arrange something for ¥5,000 per head?

**Restaurant:** ¥5,000. . . I see. What about drinks?

**Nakamura:** Drinks can be extra.

**Restaurant:** Drinks, separate. . . What time can we expect you?

**Nakamura:** About 6:30 I think.

**Restaurant:** Very well. We’ll be expecting you. . .
