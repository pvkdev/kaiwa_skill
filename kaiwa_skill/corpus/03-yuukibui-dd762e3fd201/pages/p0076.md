## Key Dialogue tiếp theo (biên tập)

**Librarian:** Library.

**Chang:** Excuse me, but can anyone use your library?

**Librarian:** Yes. If you are of college student age(or older), everyone is welcome.

**Chang:** Can foreigners use it, too?

**Librarian:** Yes, certainly. What country are you from?

**Chang:** Well, I’m a Chinese student studying Japanese at Nichibei Kaiwa Gakuin.

**Librarian:** Oh, is that so? Students from that school have used our facilities.

**Chang:** What are the library hours?

**Librarian:** On weekdays it’s 9:45 a.m. till 4:45 p.m. However, we are closed from 12:00 to 1:00 for lunch. And on Saturdays, we’re open till noon.

**Chang:** I see. What kind of reading newspapers do you have from China?

**Librarian:** Recently we have started getting local papers. I suppose we have close to 20 newspapers for China alone.

**Chang:** Is that so? Do you also get the Keizai Nippō?

**Librarian:** Yes, we do.

**Chang:** I see. Thank you very much.

**Librarian:** You’re welcome.
