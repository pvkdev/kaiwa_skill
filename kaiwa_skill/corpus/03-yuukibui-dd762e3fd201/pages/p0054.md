## Variation 3: A year-end gift (A mid-summer gift)

A: an employee  
B: Kachō, (section chief)

A sends a gift to his section chief in appreciation for his help and guidance.

**A:** Excuse me Chief.

**B:** What is it?

**A:** Well, yesterday I asked Isetan Department Store to deliver a small present to you, so please accept it. I didn’t think it was a good idea to hand it to you in the office. . .

**B:** What in the world are you talking about! You don’t have to be so formal with me.

**A:** Oh, no! It’s just a small token. . . (lit. I’m always indebted to you.)

**B:** Oh, really? Thank you very much. Then I accept it with thanks.

**A:** I know I should really visit your home to express my gratitude properly, but. . .

**B:** Oh no, no. Just the thought is enough.

**A:** Yes, thank you. (lit. I appreciate all you’ve done for me in the past, and I politely request your assistance now and also in the future.)
