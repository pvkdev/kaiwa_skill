## Key Dialogue tiếp theo (biên tập)

**Wane:** Mr. Taniguchi is out at the moment.

**Ōbayashi:** Oh, he’s out? What time do you expect him back?

**Wane:** He’s supposed to be back around 4:00. Excuse me, but could you tell me what the call is about?

**Ōbayashi:** Actually, I wanted to ask him a few questions...but never mind. You expect him about 4:00? Then I’ll call again about that time.

**Wane:** Shall I have Mr. Taniguchi call you back when he returns?

**Ōbayashi:** That’s a good idea. Please have him do so.

**Wane:** Excuse me, but could I have your phone number just in case?

**Ōbayashi:** Yes, it’s 391-5231, extension 321. Ōbayashi.

**Wane:** 391-5231, extension 321, Mr. Ōbayashi. I’ll have him call you back when he returns. This is Wane speaking.

**Ōbayashi:** Mr. Wane? Please kindly relay the message.

**Wane:** Yes, I’ll take care of it. Good-bye.

**Ōbayashi:** Good-bye.

**Wane:** Good-bye.

(Wait for the caller to hang up first.)

## Variation 1: When the person requested isn’t at his desk

A: Yamada, the person who places the call

B: the person who receives the call

**A:** Hello, this is Yamada. Is Mr. Taniguchi there?

**B:** I’m afraid he’s not at his desk at the moment.

**A:** Oh, really? (That’s too bad)

**B:** Shall I take a message?
