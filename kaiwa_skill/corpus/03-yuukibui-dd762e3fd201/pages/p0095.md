## Key Dialogue tiếp theo (biên tập)

**Marcel:** I understand very well.

**Buchō:** It might seem like I’m cautioning you about a very small thing, but please don’t take it badly.

**Marcel:** Yes, thank you very much for the warning. I know that when I’m with Japanese, especially my superiors, I have to avoid addressing them with “san”, and also that I have to avoid doing things like sitting on a desk, but I hadn’t realized THAT. . .

**Buchō:** Well, you can’t be expected to learn everything at once.

**Marcel:** Yes, I know. If you notice something else, please let me know.
