## Lesson 1　Self-Introduction

### Objectives

In this lesson you will practice how to introduce yourself. Think about what would be the most appropriate things to talk about in this situation.

<a id="p0008-b002"></a>
### Key Dialogue: Self-introduction

Vichai Pramote: a Thai trainee at Tōyō Shōji

Mr. Pramote is studying at a Japanese language school. He introduces himself at the first lesson.

My name is Vichai Pramote. I’m a trainee at Tōyō Shōji. I’m from Chiang Mai in Thailand. In Thailand the name “Pramote” is as common as Smith in the U.S., or Suzuki in Japan.

Chiang Mai is well known as a resort. I wonder if any of you have heard of it?

In Thailand I was working in the Export Section of Thai Tōyō, but I got the chance to receive some training in the head office in Tōkyō. I studied Japanese when I was in college, but it still isn’t enough. I believe that to improve my Japanese I’ll need to get a lot of practice speaking. That’s why I came to this school.

Although I’ve just come to Japan, I was surprised that the Tōkyō transportation system is so convenient. In addition to that I was also rather shocked by the morning and evening rush hours.

I don’t have any special hobby worth mentioning, but I like swimming. If there is anybody who likes to swim, I’d enjoy getting together and having fun.

I’m looking forward to becoming good friends with everybody here.

JLPT Mogi

Yuuki Bui
