## Variation 2: Asking about the meaning of a word

**A:** What does “O.A.” mean?

**B:** “O.A.” is an abbreviation of “office automation”, and means the use of computers for all kinds of office work.

<a id="p0012-b002"></a>
## Variation 3: Asking about words with the same sound (pronunciation)

**A:** What does FURARERU mean? I checked my dictionary, but it only defines it as “it rains” or “to wave a hand.”

**B:** What was the situation when you heard that expression?

**A:** I heard Mr. Yamashita saying, “ONNANOKO NI FURARETA.” (She jilted me.)

**B:** Oh, that means his invitation was refused by a girl. (He asked her for a date, and she refused.)

**A:** Oh, that’s too bad.

<a id="p0012-b003"></a>
## Variation 4: Loan words

**A:** In what sense is the word “smart” used in Japanese?

**B:** When somebody has a slender or beautiful figure, we often call him or her a “smart person.”

**A:** You don’t use the word in the sense of intelligent or clever?

**B:** No, generally we don’t.

<a id="p0012-b004"></a>
## Variation 5: Pronunciation of Kanji

**A:** Is BŌ in BŌEKI a long vowel?

**B:** Yes, it’s long: BŌ.
