## Key Dialogue tiếp theo (biên tập)

**Fujin:** Please come in, this way.

(Kachō’s wife shows him into the room.)

**Simon:** Sorry I was late, kachō.

**Kachō:** Hi Simon-kun, welcome! Were you able to find our house without any trouble?

**Simon:** Well, to tell the truth, I got a little lost.

**Fujin:** Please sit here.

(Kachō’s wife offers a cushion. Mr. Simon sits in the formal Japanese way.)

**Fujin:** Please relax and sit comfortably.

**Simon:** Ah! Thank you. Excuse me, then.

**Kachō:** First let’s have a toast.

〔all〕 Cheers!

**Simon:** ITADAKIMASU.

**Kachō:** Simon-kun is a strong drinker, so keep his glass full.

**Simon:** No, I’m not much of a drinker.

**Kachō:** These are my wife’s specialities. Please don’t hesitate to eat.

**Fujin:** Please eat a lot. I hope you like it.

**Simon:** It all looks delicious. I will eat a lot.

(Mr. Simon tries to serve himself some food using the opposite end of the chopsticks.)

**Kachō:** Don’t bother. Just use them the regular way.

**Simon:** Well, if you don’t mind. . .

(After a while)

**Fujin:** Mr. Simon, don’t be modest. Please have more to eat.

**Simon:** No thank you. I’ve already eaten so much that I can hardly move.

(Mr. Simon starts to carry some dishes to the kitchen.)

**Fujin:** Oh, Mr. Simon, just leave them.

**Simon:** Well, I feel bad just eating (and not helping you).

(A verbal exchange when one leaves after dinner.)

**Simon:** Kachō, I’m sure I’ve overstayed my welcome. I’d better get going.
