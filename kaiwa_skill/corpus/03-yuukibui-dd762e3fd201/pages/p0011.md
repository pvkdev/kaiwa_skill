## Lesson 2　Asking For Clarification

### Objectives

There are occasions when we cannot understand the meaning of certain words, or read a Kanji, or know what is the correct pronunciation for a word. You will practice the forms of speech to use in such cases.

<a id="p0011-b002"></a>
### Key Dialogue: Asking about the reading and meaning of Kanji

Nozaki: an employee

John: a foreign employee and colleague of Mr. Nozaki at the same company

**John:** Excuse me, Mr. Nozaki, I have a question.

**Nozaki:** Yes, what is it?

**John:** How do you read this Kanji?

Chữ trong khung (biên tập): 騒音

**Nozaki:** Ah, that. It’s read SŌON.

**John:** SŌON? What does SŌON mean?

**Nozaki:** SŌON means noise (bothersome or irritating noise).

**John:** Ah, then for example, the television next door or the noise of a car are called SŌON?

**Nozaki:** Yes, that’s right.

**John:** I see. Thank you for taking the time to help me.

**Nozaki:** Not at all. Anytime you have any questions, please feel free to ask me.

<a id="p0011-b003"></a>
### Variation 1: Asking about the meaning of a word

**A:** What does TŌDORI mean?

**B:** TŌDORI means the head of a bank, and is equivalent to the president of a company.
