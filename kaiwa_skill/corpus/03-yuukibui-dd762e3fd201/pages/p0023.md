## Variation 4: Refusing an invitation (with a good reason)

**A:** Ō-kun, how about coming over to my house next Sunday? My wife would enjoy cooking for you.

**B:** That’s very nice of you. Next Sunday is… the 17th? I’m terribly sorry, but…

**A:** Can’t make it?

**B:** Well, I’m afraid that’s the day of my friend’s wedding and I have to attend it.

**A:** Oh, you should. Then shall we try the following Sunday?

**B:** Yes, I’d appreciate that.
