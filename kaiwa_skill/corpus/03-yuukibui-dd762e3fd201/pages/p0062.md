## Lesson 12　Apology

### Objectives

People sometimes make mistakes. In such a situation how should you apologize? You will think about that in this lesson.

<a id="p0062-b002"></a>
### Key Dialogue: Mistaking the day of an appointment

Matsumoto: a Japanese male  
Song: Matsumoto’s friend from the Republic of Korea

Mr. Matsumoto had an appointment with his friend, Mr. Song, in Shibuya. Mr. Song is late, so Mr. Matsumoto telephones him.

**Matsumoto:** Hello Mr. Song? What happened? I’ve been waiting for over 30 minutes.

**Song:** What?!

**Matsumoto:** Weren’t we supposed to meet today in Shibuya at 6 o’clock?

**Song:** Huh! Was that today? Wasn’t it supposed to be the 8th?

**Matsumoto:** No, it was the 4th. You always keep your appointments so I thought there was no way that you would be late. I phoned because I thought something had come up.

**Song:** Oh, I’m terribly sorry! I made a mistake and confused the 4th and the 8th. I suppose I should have confirmed the day of the week, huh?

**Matsumoto:** Can you leave now?

**Song:** I’m terribly sorry but some urgent business came in today. I really apologize.

**Matsumoto:** Well, I guess it can’t be helped.

**Song:** I’m sorry for wasting your valuable time. To make up for it, the next time we meet I’ll treat you (to something.)

**Matsumoto:** Oh, you needn’t worry about it.
