## Vocabulary tiếp theo (biên tập)

お先（さき）に  
〔lit.〕 excuse me for going ahead (excusing oneself for doing something before someone else)

お疲（つか）れさま（でした）  
It must have been hard work (after the hard work, sharing the tired feeling with colleagues or superiors) (cf. ごくろうさまでした is used only towards one’s inferior and should not be used towards one’s superior.)

p.31

ごいっしょする  
to accompany someone (humble)

やっぱり  
after all

やる
