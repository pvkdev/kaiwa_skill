**Sawada:** In peak seasons we often work all night. Young people don’t like to work late hours. What is your feeling about that?

**Ken:** If it’s business it can’t be helped.

**Sawada:** Excuse me for asking a personal question, but is your wife Japanese?

**Ken:** No, American. However, she has a job too, and I think she’ll understand.

**Sawada:** About pay. The base salary is 420,000 yen including taxes. In addition there will be allowances.

**Ken:** What do you mean by allowances?

**Sawada:** For example, a housing allowance, though it’s only a drop in the bucket, and a commuting allowance and overtime pay. The contract is renewable every two years. Also, you can use the company vacation resorts.

**Ken:** That’s good.

**Sawada:** Well, that’s about it. Do you have any questions?

**Ken:** No, not at the moment.

**Sawada:** Well then, we will send you notification by mail within this week.

**Ken:** Thank you very much.
