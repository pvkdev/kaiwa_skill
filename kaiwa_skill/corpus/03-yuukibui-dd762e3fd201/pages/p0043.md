## Variation: Using the company’s vacation house

A: a foreign employee

B: a member of the General Affairs Department.

A wants to use the company’s vacation facility and makes inquiries with a person in the General Affairs Department.

**A:** I’d like to use the company vacation house during the next series of holidays. Are there any vacancies? I understand there is a place in Hakone.

**B:** Hakone is a popular place so you’ll have to draw lots.

**A:** Are there any other places available?

**B:** In Karuizawa and Izu.

**A:** Oh, really. There is one in Karuizawa?

**B:** If you want to use it, fill out the application form. There is space available in Karuizawa.

**A:** Is that so? Then I’ll bring it (application form) today. Thank you very much.
