## Key Dialogue tiếp theo (biên tập)

Japan.

**Ken:** Yes, I worked on product brochures and catalogues.

**Sawada:** I believe I asked you on the phone yesterday but, did you bring some of your work?

**Ken:** Yes, I did. These. . .

**Sawada:** Please let me see. Ah. . .These are brochures for IC testers, aren’t they?

**Ken:** Yes, that’s right.

**Sawada:** May I keep them for one week?

**Ken:** Yes, of course.

**Sawada:** You’re working now but why did you apply for this job?

**Ken:** I’m teaching English now but I really want to use my professional knowledge. Besides that, I can make the best use of my creativity and inventive ability in copy writing.

**Sawada:** Yes, I see. . . What do you think is the most important thing for a copywriter?

**Ken:** Well I suppose you have to be fond of words (language).

**Sawada:** What do you mean by “fond of words”?

**Ken:** To have an interest in all kinds of language. Also in movies, fashion, sports and technical vocabulary. . .

**Sawada:** How do you study such “words” or “language”?

**Ken:** By reading technical magazines, but at the same time going to movies, reading what the young people are reading. . .

**Sawada:** Then, the language of the young is the main thing?

**Ken:** Yes, but it depends on the product. There are a lot of products aimed at youth.

**Sawada:** Do you plan the layout yourself?

**Ken:** No, there are specialists for that. I talk a lot with them.
