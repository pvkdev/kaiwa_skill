## Variation 1: Going back to one’s home country

A: a foreign trainee from Sri Lanka

B: Yamada, A’s superior.

A is returning to his country and goes to say goodbye to his superior, Mr. Yamada.

**A:** Mr. Yamada, I’m going back to Sri Lanka next month.

**B:** Is that so? Has it already been 2 years? Time certainly flies.

**A:** Thank you for everything.

**B:** No, no. Thank you! I couldn’t do much for you.

**A:** That’s not true. You taught me all kinds of things about the way Japanese business works, and it helped me a lot. Be sure to let me know if you’re going to come to Sri Lanka.

**B:** I will. Take care, and good luck.

**A:** Thank you. You too.
