## Lesson 5　Asking Directions

### Objectives

In this chapter you will study how to ask for directions when you don’t know how to get someplace, and also learn how to give directions to someone else.

<a id="p0025-b002"></a>
### Key Dialogue: Directions to Kachō’s house

Kachō: a section chief

Simon: a foreign employee

Simon will visit the section chief’s home, but he doesn’t know how to get there. So he asks the section chief.

**Simon:** How can I get to your house?

**Kachō:** I’ll draw a map for you. You get out of the north exit of Asagaya Station on the Chūō line.

**Simon:** Is there just one ticket gate?

**Kachō:** Yes, only one ticket gate, but there are two exits, north and south. After you go out the north exit you’ll see Mitsubishi Bank on the right.

**Simon:** Mitsubishi Bank?

**Kachō:** Yes. There’s a street between the bank and a coffee shop called “Ron”. You go straight down it. After 100 meters or so, you’ll see a sushi shop on the left-hand corner. You turn left there. A little further on you’ll end up in front of a hospital.

**Simon:** If it’s a hospital it should be easy to recognize, right?

**Kachō:** Yes, it’s a fairly big hospital. Then the street will branch into two. You take the right, and then turn right at the second corner. Then if you walk straight you’ll come to my house. It’s an old two-story house.
