## Lesson 11　Leaving A Message

### Objectives

There are situations when you are either unable to keep an appointment, or will be late. In these situations it is necessary to telephone the company or person being inconvenienced and explain the situation. In this lesson you will learn how to telephone and explain the necessary circumstances.

<a id="p0057-b002"></a>
### Key Dialogue: Phoning the company from outside

Yamada: a member of sales section, Yokoyama Bussan  
Connelly: a foreign colleague

Connelly went to another company on business, but realizes that he’ll return to his office later than he expected. He phones his colleague to say that he’ll be late.

**Yamada:** Sales section, Yokoyama Bussan.

**Connelly:** This is Connelly. Is that Mr. Nakamura?

**Yamada:** Oh, Mr. Connelly! This is Yamada. Mr. Nakamura is out just now. Is there anything I can do?

**Connelly:** Well actually, I’m at Santō Shōkai about that matter. It took a bit longer (than I expected). I’ve just finished and will go on to Unitech Shōkai. If something comes up could you please ask Mr. Nakamura to contact me there?

**Yamada:** Yes, I see. You’ll be at Unitech.

**Connelly:** Yes, I’ll be with Mr. Fran of the Sales Department.

**Yamada:** And about what time can you get back?

**Connelly:** Well, it may take quite a while. If it’s late I’ll go on home instead of going back to the company.

**Yamada:** I see. If you’re going straight home, please phone us, Mr. Nakamura may have something for you.

**Connelly:** Yes, I understand.
