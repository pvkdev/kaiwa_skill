## Lesson 15　Making Inquiries

### Objectives

This lesson is concerned with practice for using the phone in order to obtain informa-  
tion, especially concerning social events, and also the use of facilities.

<a id="p0075-b002"></a>
### Key Dialogue: Asking how to use the library

Operator  
Chang: a Chinese student studying Japanese  
Librarian: a librarian of the institute

Mr. Chang phones NTT Inquiries to ask for the telephone number of the Institute of Asian Economic Affairs.

**Operator:** This is NTT Inquiries.

**Chang:** Excuse me, but what number is the Institute of Asian Economic Affairs?

**Operator:** Institute of Asian Economic Affairs?

**Chang:** Yes, that’s right.

**Operator:** Just a moment please.

**Operator:** Thank you for waiting. The number is 353-4231.

**Chang:** 353-4231? Thank you.

**Operator:** You’re welcome.

(He phones about using the library attached to the institute.)

**Operator:** Institute of Asian Economic Affairs.

**Chang:** Connect me to the library please.

**Operator:** Just a moment please.

JLPT Mogi

Yuuki Bui
