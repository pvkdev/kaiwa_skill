## Lesson 17　Honorific Form (Polite Form)

### Objectives

There are many situations when the honorific form of speech must be used. In this lesson you will practice how to use various polite forms of speech.

<a id="p0084-b002"></a>
### Key Dialogue: At a company anniversary party

Smith: Tōkyō representative of a Canadian pharmaceutical company.  
Noda: department manager of a certain company.

The Tōkyō branch of Mr. Smith’s company is celebrating its 25th anniversary this month. Mr. Noda has been invited to the party also.

**Waiter:** Drinks, sir?

**Noda:** Thank you. What is this?

**Waiter:** Gin and tonic, sir.

**Noda:** Do you have scotch and water?

**Waiter:** Yes, sir. If you’ll wait a moment, I’ll bring one right away, sir.

**Noda:** Thank you, please.

**Noda:** Well, well. If it isn’t Mr. Smith. It’s been a long time.

**Smith:** Oh, Mr. Noda. Sorry, I hadn’t noticed you.

**Noda:** Not at all. Thank you for inviting me today.

**Smith:** Thank you for coming at such a busy time for you.

**Noda:** It’s quite a party, isn’t it? So it’s 25 years since you opened your office in Tōkyō?

**Smith:** Thanks to you (people like yourself), we are able to celebrate our 25th year.
