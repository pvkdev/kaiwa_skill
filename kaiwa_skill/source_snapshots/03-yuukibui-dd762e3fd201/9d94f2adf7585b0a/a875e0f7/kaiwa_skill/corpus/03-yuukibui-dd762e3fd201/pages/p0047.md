## Lesson 9　Reservations

### Objectives

This chapter provides practice in making reservations for hotels, restaurants, buying tickets, et cetera.

<a id="p0047-b002"></a>
### Key Dialogue: At a travel agency

Nelson: a male visitor  
Employee: an employee of a travel agency

Mr. Nelson is going to Matsumoto, Shinshu on a business trip. He goes to a travel agency to reserve train tickets.

**Nelson:** Excuse me. I’d like to make a reservation.

**Employee:** If it’s ticket reservations, I can take care of you.

**Nelson:** Is that so? Well it’s for Tuesday, July 28th. I want two reserved seats for the 9:00 a.m. limited express for Matsumoto city in Shinshū.

**Employee:** July 28th. That’s next Tuesday, isn’t it? Two tickets on the 9:00 a.m. limited express for Matsumoto. Round-trip or one way?

**Nelson:** One way will do.

**Employee:** That’s one way, right? What shall we do if they’re sold out? The next train is at 10:00 a.m. Will that do?

**Nelson:** That’s kind of late. If they’re sold out then the Green tickets (first class) will do.

**Employee:** In this season the Green tickets are actually harder to get.

**Nelson:** Well in that case we’ll have to line up for the unreserved seats.
