## Variation: Meeting someone or arranging to meet someone at a place

A: Simon

B: Takahashi, Simon’s colleague

Takahashi and Simon decide on a place to meet in order to visit their section chief’s home.

**A:** Where shall we meet, Mr. Takahashi?

**B:** Let’s see, where is a good place? You use the Odakyū line, don’t you, Simon? Since, Kachō’s house is in Asagaya, how about meeting in Shinjuku? Shinjuku Station is complicated, so why don’t we meet on the J.R. platform?

**A:** O.K. What’s the platform number?

**B:** The far end of platform number 12 will be easier.

**A:** Which end? The Yoyogi end or the Nakano end?

**B:** Let’s make it the Yoyogi end. Since the appointment is for one o’clock, how about meeting there at 12 noon?

**A:** All right. Is number 12 the express platform?

**B:** No, tomorrow is Sunday, so the express won’t stop at Asagaya. It’s the Sōbu line platform.

**A:** The trains on the Sōbu line are yellow, aren’t they?

**B:** That’s right. Be careful you don’t make a mistake.

**A:** So it’s platform number 12 at 12 noon.
