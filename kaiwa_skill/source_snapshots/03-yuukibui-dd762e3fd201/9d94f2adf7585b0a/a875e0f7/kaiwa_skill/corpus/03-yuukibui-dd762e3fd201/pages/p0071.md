## Key Dialogue tiếp theo (biên tập)

**Jones:** Especially the transactions. I hear it’s a very complicated process till one signs the contract.

**Shachō:** Yes it is. I want you to really study that part well. By the way, is the Research and Development Manager, Mr. Rockhart, well?

**Jones:** Yes, he seemed very well.

**Shachō:** When I went to England he went to a lot of trouble for me. He’s a very friendly person.

**Jones:** Yes. He’s a wonderful manager. He has been put in charge of the Middle East.

**Shachō:** Really, the Middle East! That’s a difficult area but then he’s a very capable person. . .Well, things may be a bit confusing but please do your best.

**Jones:** Thank you.

**Kachō:** Good-bye.

## Variation 1: Talking about a mutual acquaintance

**A:** By the way, how is Mr. Jiratiwat of Bangkok Shōkai?

**B:** Mr. Jiratiwat? I haven’t seen him recently. I don’t know the details but I understand that he started a new business.

**A:** Oh, really? I didn’t know that!

<a id="p0071-b003"></a>
## Variation 2: Conveying a message from the manager

**A:** Mr. B, are you acquainted with Mr. Bernard of the East Asia Department?

**B:** Well, I’ve never met him but I always hear a lot about him.

**A:** Well, he’s coming to Japan this fall and expressed a strong desire to meet you and talk with you then.

**B:** Really? I look forward to it.
