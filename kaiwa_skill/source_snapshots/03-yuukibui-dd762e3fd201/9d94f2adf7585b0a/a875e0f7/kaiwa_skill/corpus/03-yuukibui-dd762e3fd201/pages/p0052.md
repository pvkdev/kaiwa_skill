## Lesson 10　A Souvenir

### Objectives

In this lesson you will study the customs for gift-giving in Japan, and practice the appropriate expressions related to them.

<a id="p0052-b002"></a>
### Key Dialogue: After taking a few days off

Kachō: a section chief  
Sha: an employee  
Nakano: a female employee

Mr. Sha has just come back from Taiwan where he had attended a wedding. He has brought back some souvenirs for his section chief and his colleagues.

**Sha:** I’m afraid I’ve inconvenienced you.

**Kachō:** Welcome back. How was Taiwan?

**Sha:** Fortunately the weather was good. Everything went well.

**Kachō:** I’m glad. I hear weddings in Taiwan are impressive affairs. I suppose many people return for a wedding in the family?

**Sha:** Yes, the Chinese go all out for weddings and the New Year celebrations. Although compared with the old days things have been simplified. I’m sorry for taking five days off at such a busy time.

**Kachō:** That’s all right. After all, it was a wedding.

**Sha:** Well. . .This is just a trifle, it’s oolong tea.

**Kachō:** That’s very kind of you. You needn’t have taken the trouble.

**Sha:** It’s just a token of appreciation.

**Kachō:** Thanks. We’ll all have some together.

**Sha:** No, that’s all right. I have some for everyone else. . . Ms. Nakano, here’s some oolong tea for you.
