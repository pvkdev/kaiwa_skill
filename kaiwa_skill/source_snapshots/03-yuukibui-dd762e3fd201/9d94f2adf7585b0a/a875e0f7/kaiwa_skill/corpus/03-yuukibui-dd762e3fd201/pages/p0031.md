## Variation 2: Business transfer

A: an employee

B: A’s superior.

Congratulating and saying goodbye to one’s superior, who has been promoted to a post overseas.

**A:** Kachō, congratulations on your new post. I heard you’re going to Mexico.

**B:** Yes, I’ll be there for 2 years or so. It’s going to be hard, because I’m afraid I forgot all the Spanish I studied.

**A:** I don’t think you have to worry. Are you taking all of your family along?

**B:** No, I’m afraid not. My eldest child is taking the entrance exams next year. So we decided to leave him/her with my relatives.

**A:** Then, you’ll miss him/her.

**B:** Well, it’s one of these things. Please take care of things at the office.

**A:** Of course. I’ll do my best so don’t worry about anything.

## Variation 3: Meeting after 5 years

A: Julie

B: Sakamoto, Julie’s former teacher

Julie happens to meet her former teacher, Mr. Sakamoto, after a long time.

**A:** Mr. Sakamoto! Mr. Sakamoto!

**B:** Ah, Julie, how have you been?

**A:** It’s been a long time. How are you?

**B:** I’m fine, thank you. The same as always.
