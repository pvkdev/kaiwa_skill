## Variation 1: Changing the date of an appointment

A had an appointment to visit B’s company. He finds that he is suddenly unable to go. He telephones B to have the appointment changed.

**A:** I was supposed to visit you today but the fact is, that there was an accident in our Tsurumi factory last night. I will have to go to the factory to investigate the cause.

**B:** That’s too bad.

**A:** And I’m not sure what time I can get back. I apologize for making this request suddenly but can you change the day of our meeting?

**B:** Yes, that’s fine. When things are settled please telephone me. At that time we’ll decide the day of the meeting once more.

**A:** I’m terribly sorry.

**B:** No, no. These things cannot be helped. Don’t let it bother you.

## Variation 2: Losing something one has borrowed

A: a man  
B: Ms. Sato, A’s friend

A has left the book he borrowed from B (Ms. Sato) in the train. A apologizes to B.

**A:** Ms. Sato, I don’t know how to say this, but I’ve done a terrible thing.

**B:** What on earth has happened?

**A:** It’s about the book I borrowed from you the other day. I’ve inadvertently left it in the train.

**B:** What! You forgot it?

**A:** I’m very sorry. I left it on the rack in the train. I did contact the Lost and Found department of the station, but. . .

**B:** It’s not much of a book. You needn’t worry.
