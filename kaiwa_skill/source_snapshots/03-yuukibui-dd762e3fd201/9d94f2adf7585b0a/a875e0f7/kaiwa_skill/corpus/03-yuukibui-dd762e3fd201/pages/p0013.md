## Variation 5 tiếp theo (biên tập)

**A:** Is it BŌ or PŌ?

**B:** It’s BŌ. There are two dots. (which indicate a voiced sound)

## Variation 6: Pronunciation of Kanji

Chữ trong khung (biên tập): 目上

**A:** Is this MEJŌ?

**B:** No, this is read MEUE. Kanji compounds are usually a combination of “KUN reading and KUN reading” or “ON reading and ON reading.” Of course there are some exceptions.

<a id="p0013-b003"></a>
## Variation 7: How to read someone’s name

**A:** (receiving a business card) Excuse me, but how should I read your name?

**B:** Usually this is read “Watanabe”, but in my case it is “Watabe”. Even Japanese people often make mistakes with it.

Chữ trong khung (biên tập): 渡部

**A:** I see. So you’re Mr. Watabe.

<a id="p0013-b004"></a>
## Variation 8: Asking the meaning of a sentence

**A:** (Showing B a piece of paper with an advertisement on it) What does this advertising message mean? Here it is. It says, “Nannari to gosōdan, goyōmei kudasaimasuyō onegai mōshiagemasu. (We’ll be happy to meet any of your needs, so please feel free to consult us on anything.)”

**B:** Oh, that. This is an expression often used by companies or shops, and it appeals to customers, asking them to seek out advice on anything, or ask anything, and stressing that they’ll do their best to help.
