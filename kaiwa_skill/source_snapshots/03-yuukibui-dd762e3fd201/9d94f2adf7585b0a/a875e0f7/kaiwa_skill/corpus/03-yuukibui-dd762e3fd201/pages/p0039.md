## Variation 6: Confirming the name of the caller

**B:** Who is calling, please?

**A:** This is Ōkita.

**B:** Excuse me but would you mind saying it again?

**A:** Ōkita.

**B:** Mr. Okita?

**A:** No, Ōkita.

**B:** Excuse me. Mr. Ōkita. Is that correct?
