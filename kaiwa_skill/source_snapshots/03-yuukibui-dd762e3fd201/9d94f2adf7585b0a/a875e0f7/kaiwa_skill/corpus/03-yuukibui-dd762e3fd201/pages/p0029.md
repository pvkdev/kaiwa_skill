## Lesson 6　Greetings

### Objectives

This lesson covers various daily greeting patterns, such as congratulating people, greeting those who are newly transferred, and also introducing yourself when you move into a new place.

<a id="p0029-b002"></a>
### Key Dialogue: Daily greetings at the office in the morning and evening

Kachō: a section chief

Raman: a foreign employee

Matsushita: a female employee, Raman’s colleague

(morning)

**Raman:** Good morning, Kachō.

**Kachō:** Good morning.

**Raman:** Thank you for treating me last night.

**Kachō:** Did you go straight home after that?

**Raman:** To tell the truth, I stopped off for another drink. I’m afraid I drank a bit too much.

**Matsushita:** Good morning, Kachō.

**Kachō:** Good morning.

**Matsushita:** Good morning, Mr. Raman. (serving tea to the two) Have some tea and clear your heads. I made it extra strong. Drink it before it gets cold.

**Kachō:** Thanks. It’s good. The tea you make is the world’s best.

(evening)

**Kachō:** Oh, it’s already 7 o’clock. It’s about time to quit. Raman-kun, won’t you join me again tonight?
