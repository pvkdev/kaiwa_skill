## Key Dialogue tiếp theo (biên tập)

**Kachō:** It’s only 9 o’clock. You can stay a little longer, can’t you?

**Fujin:** Yes, please stay longer.

**Simon:** Thank you, but since I live far from here, I’d better get going. Thank you for a wonderful meal.

**Kachō:** Well, if you insist. . .

**Fujin:** I’m afraid it wasn’t much of a meal, but please be sure to come again.

**Simon:** Thank you. Next time please come to my home. It’s not spacious, but I’d like you to try some food from my country. Please come along with your husband, Okusan.

**Kachō:** We’ll be looking forward to it then.

**Simon:** Good night.

**Kachō:** Good night.

**Fujin:** Have a safe trip home. Good night.

## Variation 1: Praising food

(1) **A:** This is light and delicious. I could eat a lot of it.

**B:** Please eat as much as you like. I’m glad you like it.

**A:** Is it hard to cook? Could you give me the recipe? I’d like to try it myself.

(2) **A:** It looks so beautiful that it’s a shame to destroy it by eating it.

**B:** It’s often said that Japanese food is eaten with the eyes.

**A:** “Eaten with the eyes.” That’s an interesting expression. These plates and bowls are beautiful, too. The colors of the food and the dishes match beautifully.
