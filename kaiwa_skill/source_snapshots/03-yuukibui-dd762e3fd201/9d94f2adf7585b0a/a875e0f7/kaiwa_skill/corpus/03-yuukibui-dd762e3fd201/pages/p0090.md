## Key Dialogue tiếp theo (biên tập)

**Okuyama:** I’m not certain that I can help you, but I’m willing to hear you out.

**Bell:** I would greatly appreciate that. Well then, rather than talking about it on the phone, I’d appreciate it if you would allow me to meet you and explain directly in person.

**Okuyama:** That will be fine. Let’s see, when should we meet? I suppose the sooner the better for you?

**Bell:** Yes. Before meeting you I’d like to send you a brochure describing the things we’d like to market in Japan. I’d appreciate it if you would read it.

**Okuyama:** Certainly.

**Bell:** Well then, when we meet I’d appreciate it if you could give me your opinion (on our products).

**Okuyama:** Yes, I understand.

**Bell:** I’ll have it delivered immediately by messenger service.

**Okuyama:** Well then, when would you like to meet?

**Bell:** Anytime is all right for me. When would it be convenient for you, Mr. Okuyama?

**Okuyama:** Just a moment please. . . I’m free this Friday from three o’clock.

**Bell:** Wonderful! Would three o’clock be all right to call on you?

**Okuyama:** Yes, fine, at three o’clock. I’ll get some information ready by then. Do you know how to get here? It is a bit complicated from the station.

**Bell:** I know how. I’ve passed in front (of your office) once.

**Okuyama:** Is that so? Then I’ll expect you at three o’clock.

**Bell:** Good-bye. (I’m looking forward to receiving any help you can give me.)
