## Lesson 13　Asking About Japanese Customs And Expressions

### Objectives

Depending on the country, customs and expressions are different. In this lesson, you will practice how to ask Japanese people about these differences.

<a id="p0066-b002"></a>
### Key Dialogue 1: OTSUKARESAMA DE GOZAIMASHITA

John: a foreign employee  
Yamazaki: a Japanese colleague

John is not certain about greeting customs in Japan. He asks Mr. Yamazaki about the correct usage of terms.

**John:** Mr. Yamazaki, I’ve got a question if you have a minute?

**Yamazaki:** Yes, what is it?

**John:** What should I say when the section chief leaves the office for home?

**Yamazaki:** Let’s see. You get up from your seat, bow, and say “OTSUKARESAMA DE GOZAIMASHITA.” I think that would be good enough.

**John:** I see. Thank you very much.

Note: It’s better not to use “GOKUROSAMA” or “OTSUKARESAMA” to a superior. However, if you can’t find any other suitable expression then “OTSUKARESAMA DE GOZAIMASHITA” could be used towards a superior.

### Key Dialogue 2: Seasonal greetings used in letters

John is trying to write a letter of thanks in Japanese. He is not sure whether he has to start with a seasonal greeting.
