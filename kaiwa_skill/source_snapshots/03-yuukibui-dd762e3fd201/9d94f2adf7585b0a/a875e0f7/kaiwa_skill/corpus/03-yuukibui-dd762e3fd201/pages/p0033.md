## Variation 6: Condolence

A: an employee

B: Kachō (section chief), A’s superior

A is offering condolences to his superior who has just lost his father.

**A:** Kachō, I was very sorry to hear the news. I was shocked when I heard it during *my* business trip.

**B:** Yes, it was sudden.

**A:** How old was he?

**B:** He was 81.

**A:** I’m sure you will miss him.

**B:** Yes.
