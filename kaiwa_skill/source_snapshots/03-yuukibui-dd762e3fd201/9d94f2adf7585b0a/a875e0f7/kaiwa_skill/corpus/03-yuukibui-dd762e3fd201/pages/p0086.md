## Key Dialogue tiếp theo (biên tập)

**Smith:** You’re being modest. How about a game one of these days?

**Noda:** Oh! You also play? Well, then, I’ll telephone you one day and invite you.

**Smith:** I look forward to it. Oh! Will you excuse me for a moment? I see some new guests.

**Noda:** Please go ahead. Please don’t mind me.

**Smith:** Please make yourself at home. Excuse me.
