## Key Dialogue 2 tiếp theo (biên tập)

**John:** I got a birthday present from my guarantor, and I’d like to write a thank-you note. But when I checked some reference books I found a lot of complicated information. Is it rude if you don’t write “seasonal greetings”?

**Yamazaki:** Well, you don’t have to use a difficult expression, but I think it’s better to include it.

**John:** How should I put it?

**Yamazaki:** Let me think. Something like,“At last we’re having warm spring weather. How are you?” That would do for this time of the year.

**John:** If that’s all I have to write, I guess I can manage it.

## Variation: A return gift

John: a foreign employee  
Yamazaki: a Japanese colleague

John has received a birthday present from Mr. Yamamoto, his section chief. He asks Mr. Yamazaki whether he should prepare a gift in return.

**John:** I received a birthday present from my section chief, Mr. Yamamoto. Should I give him something in return?

**Yamazaki:** No, I don’t think it’s necessary, though some people give a gift in return immediately. However, it’s bad to only be on the receiving end, so maybe you could find some occasion like when you travel somewhere, or on a holiday like Christmas, to give something in return.
