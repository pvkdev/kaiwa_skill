## Variation 1: Sold out

A: a customer  
B: a booking office clerk

The seat that customer A wanted is sold out but he persists in trying to get one.

**B:** Advanced tickets for the 15th are all sold out.

**A:** Well then... What about the 16th?

**B:** Special Reserved seats for the 16th... I’m afraid they’re sold out too.

**A:** Then what’s left on the 16th?

**B:** Both the S and A seats are all sold out. There might be some cancelled tickets available so you should come early (on the 16th).

## Variation 2: Changing an appointment

A: a customer  
B: an employee of a travel agency

A has reserved a ticket with a travel agency. He finds that he must change the day of his departure. He phones the travel agency.

**A:** I have reservations on September 27th but something has come up suddenly for *that day. Can I have it changed to a later day?*

**B:** The business day is over but let me hear what you want. I’ll tell the man in charge first thing tomorrow. (Asks passenger’s name and the phone number.)
