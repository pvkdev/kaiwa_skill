## Variation 1: Phoning to inform someone that you may be late

**A:** I’m headed your way but there was an accident on the toll road and it’s terribly congested.

**B:** Oh, that’s too bad.

**A:** So I don’t think I can meet you at the appointed time. . . If you don’t mind, can we make it an hour later?

**B:** Yes, that’s no problem. Then will 11:00 be all right?

**A:** Yes, I’m sorry to inconvenience you when you’re so busy.

**B:** Then, we’ll expect you at 11:00.

<a id="p0058-b002"></a>
## Variation 2: Notifying someone that you will be absent

**A:** Is that Mr. Uchiyama? This is Smith.

**B:** Oh, it’s you Mr. Smith.

**A:** Yes. Well, I’m terribly sorry but I’ve had a slight cold from last night and I have a headache this morning. I’d like to be allowed to take today off.

**B:** That’s too bad. Take care of yourself.

**A:** I’m sorry to ask this at such a busy time. I intend to come tomorrow unless it becomes worse.

**B:** I see. I understand. If it’s very bad there’s no need to force yourself to come.

**A:** Thank you very much.

**B:** Take care.
