## Lesson 7　Receiving An Outside Call

### Objectives

The situation of speaking on the telephone is particularly difficult in a foreign language, because you can’t see the person you’re speaking to. In this lesson you will practice how to receive a phone call in various situations.

<a id="p0036-b002"></a>
### Key Dialogue: Taking a message

Ōbayashi: an employee of Universal Research Corporation

Wane: a worker at sales department of Tōyō Shōji

Mr. Ōbayashi of Universal Research Corp. calls Mr. Taniguchi, section chief of the sales section of Tōyō Shōji. He is out and Mr. Wane answers the phone.

(over the phone)

**Operator:** Hello, this is Tōyō Shōji.

**Ōbayashi:** I’d like to speak to Mr. Taniguchi of the Sales Section.

**Operator:** Could I have your name, please?

**Ōbayashi:** This is Ōbayashi of Universal Research.

**Operator:** Just a moment, please.

**Wane:** This is the Sales Section.

**Operator:** There is a call for Mr. Taniguchi from Mr. Ōbayashi of Universal Research.

**Wane:** Hello, this is the Sales Section.

**Ōbayashi:** Er...This is Ōbayashi of Universal Research.

**Wane:** Thank you for your patronage.

**Ōbayashi:** Not at all. Is Mr. Taniguchi, the Section Chief, there?
