## Lesson 16　Visiting

### Objectives

In this lesson you will learn expressions to be used when paying a visit, and when being served by your host or hostess.

<a id="p0078-b002"></a>
### Key Dialogue: At Kachō’s house

Kachō: section chief  
Fujin: Kachō’s wife  
Simon: a foreign employee, Kachō’s subordinate

Mr. Simon is invited to the house of his section chief.

(Greeting at the front door)

**Simon:** Hello.

**Fujin:** (Coming to the door) Welcome.

**Simon:** My name is Simon. I’m working in your husband’s department. He’s a very nice boss. (lit. I’m always indebted to Kachō)

**Fujin:** It’s nice of you to say that. (lit. It’s we who are indebted to you.) I’ve often heard very nice things about you.

**Simon:** Thank you for inviting me today.

**Fujin:** Well, I’m afraid I haven’t prepared anything very fancy. . . But please do come in.

**Simon:** Thank you. Excuse me. This is a small gift that I brought along.

**Fujin:** Oh, it’s nice of you to have gone to the trouble. That’s very kind of you. Thank you very much. But next time please don’t worry about bringing anything. Just bring yourself.

**Simon:** Yes, thank you.
