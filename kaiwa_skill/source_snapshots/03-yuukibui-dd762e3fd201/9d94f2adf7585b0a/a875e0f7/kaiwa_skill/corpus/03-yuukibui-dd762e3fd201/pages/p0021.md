## Lesson 4　Refusing A Request

### Objectives

There are times when you have to refuse a request or an invitation. In this lesson you will learn a number of different ways to decline something politely.

<a id="p0021-b002"></a>
### Key Dialogue: Rejecting an offer to become an English tutor

Sakurai: an employee of a certain company

Howe: a foreign employee and Sakurai’s colleague

Mr. Sakurai asks Mr. Howe to teach English to his relative.

**Sakurai:** Excuse me, Mr. Howe, but would you do me a favor?

**Howe:** What is it?

**Sakurai:** Well, one of my relatives is a doctor, and he’s going to go to the U.S. soon. He can understand English related to his field, but he’d like to get used to ordinary spoken English before he goes. Do you think you might be able to teach him?

**Howe:** I’d like to, but I’m afraid I’m rather busy these days.

**Sakurai:** Even once a week would be enough.

**Howe:** Well, to tell you the truth, I’m going to be taking an exam for graduate school, so I’m very busy researching Asian economy. If I didn’t have anything like that I’d be happy to help you, but…

**Sakurai:** I see, you are busy. Thank you anyway.

**Howe:** Sorry I couldn’t help you.
