## Lesson 20　An Interview

### Objectives

What sort of questions are asked in a job interview? This lesson will discuss the most appropriate way to reply to such questions.

<a id="p0097-b002"></a>
### Key Dialogue: Applying for the position of a copywriter

Sawada: a member of a personnel department  
Ken: an applicant

Mr. Sawada’s company is looking for a copywriter. Ken Manheim visits the company for a job interview.

**Sawada:** Sorry to keep you waiting. I am Sawada of the personnel department.

**Ken:** How do you do? I am Ken Manheim.

**Sawada:** Please take a seat.

**Ken:** Thank you.

**Sawada:** To get right down to things, may I see your resumé?

**Ken:** Yes, certainly. Here you are.

**Sawada:** Have you been in Japan long?

**Ken:** A year and three months.

**Sawada:** I see that you’ve been teaching English all that time. Have you been studying Japanese the whole time?

**Ken:** Yes, I started right from the very beginning. . .

**Sawada:** Then you can manage Japanese all right.

**Ken:** Well, I can manage daily conversation but Kanji is very difficult. I haven’t gotten to the point yet where I can read the newspaper.
